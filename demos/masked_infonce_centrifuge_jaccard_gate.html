1. What is this model trying to do?

This model teaches a neural network to recognise which images are related. It compares one image with a matching image and with other images, while avoiding a common mistake: treating a very similar image as a negative example.

In simple words: learn what belongs together, and do not push similar land-cover examples apart by mistake.
